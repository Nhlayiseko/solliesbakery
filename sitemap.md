## Page Relationships

| Page | Filename | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Home | `index.html` | Introduces Sollie's Bakery and highlights products and calls to action. |
| About Us | `about.html` | Provides the bakery history, mission, vision and target audience. |
| Products | `products.html` | Presents bread, pastries, celebration cakes and related services. |
| Custom Orders | `enquiry.html` | Provides the custom-order and general enquiry form. |
| Contact | `contact.html` | Provides contact information, opening hours and location structure. |

## Navigation Flow

Every page contains links to:

- Home
- About Us
- Products
- Custom Orders
- Contact

This creates a consistent site-wide navigation system.

## Assignment Alignment

The Part 1 instructions require a minimum of five web pages and specify the core structure of Home, About, Products/Services, Enquiry and Contact pages. The sitemap follows that structure.
