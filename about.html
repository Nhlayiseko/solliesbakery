Our Story

Sollie's Bakery is a small ,Ffamily-run bakery estalblished in 2019 in johannesburg,South Africa.What began as a home-based baaking operation selling bread and cupcakes to neighbours has grown into a small storefrontserving fresh bread,pastries and custom celebration cakes for the local community.To date ,the bakery has relied on word-of mouth referals and a basic facebookpage to reach customers —this website marks the next step in bringing that same wormth online

Our Mission

To bring people and communities together though freshly baked,high-quality products made with locally sourced ingredientsand genuine care

Our Vision

To become the most trusted neighbourhood bakery in johannesburg, known for consistency,creativity and community involvement

Who we Serve

We proudly serve local residents,working professionals looking for convenient daily bread and pastries,Customers planning birthdays,weddings and corporate events who need custom cakes and catering
