Our Story

Sollie's Bakery is a small, family-run bakery established in 2019 in Johannesburg, South Africa. What began as a home-based baking operation selling bread and cupcakes to neighbours has grown into a storefront serving fresh bread, pastries and custom celebration cakes for the local community. To date, the bakery has relied on word-of-mouth referrals and a basic Facebook page to reach customers — this website marks the next step in bringing that same warmth online.

Our Mission

To bring people and communities together through freshly baked, high-quality products made with locally sourced ingredients and genuine care.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted neighbourhood bakery in Johannesburg, known for consistency, creativity and community involvement.

Who We Serve

We proudly serve local residents, working professionals looking for convenient daily bread and pastries, and customers planning birthdays, weddings, and corporate events who need custom cakes and catering.

What We Value
