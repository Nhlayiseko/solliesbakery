Welcome to Sollie's Bakery

Sollie's Bakery is a small, family-run bakery established in 2019 in Johannesburg, South Africa. What began as a home-based baking operation selling bread and cupcakes to neighbours has grown into a storefront serving fresh bread, pastries and custom celebration cakes for the local community.
